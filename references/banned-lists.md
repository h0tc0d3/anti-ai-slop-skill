## Adverbs

Cut every -ly word, plus really, just, literally, genuinely, honestly, simply, actually, deeply, truly, fundamentally, inherently, inevitably.

## Vague Declaratives

Announce importance without naming the thing. Cut or specify: the reasons are structural · the implications are significant · this is the deepest problem · the stakes are high · the consequences are real.
